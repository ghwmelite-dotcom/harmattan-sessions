# Release Strategy — Reference

How to release Harmattan Sessions music for maximum impact across YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, and editorial pickups.

## The Release Calendar Framework

### Weekly Cadence (Sustaining Releases)
- **Tuesday:** New YouTube compilation (30-60 min)
- **Friday:** New YouTube compilation (30-60 min)
- **Daily:** 1 Short (30-60 sec) repurposed across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram
- **Bi-weekly Sunday:** Long-form mega-mix (1-3 hours)

### Monthly Cadence (Streaming Releases)
- **First Monday of month:** New "single" (2-4 individual tracks) to Spotify/Apple via DistroKid
- **Mid-month:** Companion compilation on YouTube using those tracks + back-catalog
- **End of month:** EP-style 5-track release (every 3rd month)

### Quarterly Cadence (Tentpole Releases)
- **Quarter close:** Themed compilation album ("Harmattan: Volume X")
- **Strategic timing:** Aligned with cultural moments (Black History Month, African Liberation Day, etc.)

### Annual Cadence (Award/Campaign Releases)
- **Q1:** "Harmattan: The Album" — your award-submission collection
- **Q3:** Press tour around new compilation series
- **Q4:** Year-end "Best of" compilation

## The 3-Month Track Lifecycle

A track does NOT just "drop" once. It lives across 90 days:

### Day 0 — Strategic Drop
- Spotify release (via DistroKid, scheduled 14 days in advance)
- Apple Music + Tidal + Amazon simultaneous
- Bandcamp release (slightly different — direct sales focus)

### Day 7 — YouTube Compilation Drop
- Track included in a 30-60 min compilation
- Companion content (Shorts, IG Reels with track snippet)
- Newsletter mention

### Day 14 — Spotify Editorial Pitch
- Within 7-14 days of release, pitch via Spotify for Artists
- Submit to DailyPlaylists (free)
- Submit to SubmitHub (paid, $1-5)

### Day 30 — Cross-Platform Push
- Track appears in another compilation
- TikTok/Reels content using track snippet (8 sec hooks)
- DM to 3-5 specific creators in your niche for collab content

### Day 60 — Catalog Integration
- Track added to permanent genre playlist on your YouTube
- Track added to your Spotify artist profile sections
- Field recording footage (if any) repurposed as standalone Short

### Day 90 — Performance Review
- Analyze which platforms drove most plays
- Identify which tracks earned editorial pickup
- Iterate prompt-and-production approach for next batch

## The Spotify "Pitch a Song" Flow (Critical)

You get ONE shot per release via Spotify for Artists. Maximize it.

### Pre-Submission Checklist (Do BEFORE clicking Pitch)
- [ ] Track has been uploaded to DistroKid 14+ days before release date
- [ ] You've claimed your Spotify for Artists profile
- [ ] Artist photo, bio, and links are complete
- [ ] Cover art uploaded at correct resolution (3000×3000px)
- [ ] You've listened to 3 target playlists to confirm sonic fit

### The Pitch Itself (Fields to Fill)

**Genre tags (pick 3):**
- Primary: Afrobeats OR Lofi (depending on track)
- Secondary: Instrumental
- Tertiary: Chill OR Sleep OR Ambient (depending on sub-genre)

**Mood tags (pick 3):**
- Chill, Sunset, Romantic, Focus, Sleep, Meditative, Hopeful, Nostalgic — pick best fit

**Style tags (pick 3):**
- Instrumental, Vocal (no for our content), Acoustic, Electronic, Smooth

**Culture (pick 1):**
- African / Continental African

**Description (500 chars max):**

Template:
```
"[Track Name]" is a [genre] instrumental from Harmattan Sessions, an 
independent music project rooted in Accra, Ghana. The track blends 
[specific elements: highlife guitar / amapiano log drums / etc] with 
original field recordings captured at [specific Accra location]. 
Designed for [use case: sunset moods / study sessions / late nights]. 
Independent release, 2026. Curated chill from West Africa.
```

**Co-write/producer info:** State clearly your role (writer, producer). For AI-assisted, you can write: "Composition assisted with AI tools; mixing, curation, and field recording by Ozzy Hodges."

⚠️ **Don't lie about AI involvement.** Spotify is increasingly scrutinizing AI-generated music. Be transparent. Your transformative production layer makes you defensible.

## DistroKid Release Setup

### Initial Setup (One-Time)
1. Sign up at distrokid.com — $22.99/year unlimited uploads
2. Artist name: "Harmattan Sessions"
3. Label name: "Hodges & Co." (or leave blank — DistroKid acts as label)
4. Payment info: USD account preferred (lower fees from FX)
5. Bank: Wise/Payoneer USD account works for Ghana

### Per-Release Setup
1. Upload WAV (44.1kHz, 16-bit minimum; 48kHz, 24-bit ideal)
2. Title: Use Suno-friendly track titles ("Labadi Sunset," not "Track_001")
3. Cover art: 3000×3000px, RGB, no offensive content
4. Genre: Pick PRIMARY genre only (Afrobeat OR Electronic - Chill OR World Music)
5. Lyrics: "Instrumental" (literally just that word)
6. Explicit: NO (always — never opt-in even if a title sounds edgy)
7. Release date: 14 days minimum in future (gives editorial review time)
8. Pre-save: ENABLE (drives subscriber action before release)

### DistroKid Settings to Enable
- **YouTube Content ID:** YES (claims back royalties when others use your tracks)
- **TikTok / Instagram licensing:** YES (small extra revenue)
- **Shazam:** YES (free discovery)
- **Beatport:** YES if Coastal House releases (genre-appropriate)
- **Pandora:** YES (US-market exposure)

### DistroKid Settings to Skip
- **DistroLock:** Optional, locks tracks against takedown — useful for valuable releases
- **DistroVid:** Skip (we use YouTube directly, not DK video service)
- **Promo cards / smart links:** Free alternative is `feature.fm` (free tier)

## Smart Link Strategy (feature.fm)

Every release needs ONE shareable URL that goes everywhere:

1. Create feature.fm account (free)
2. Per release: paste Spotify URL → it auto-generates Apple/Tidal/Amazon/YouTube buttons
3. Custom URL: feature.fm/harmattan-labadi-sunset
4. Share THIS link in:
   - Instagram bio (link tree alternative)
   - YouTube descriptions
   - Email newsletter
   - DMs to playlist curators

## Bandcamp Strategy (B2B + Hardcore Fans)

Bandcamp serves different purposes than streaming:
- **Direct sales** (you keep 85-90% vs Spotify's pennies)
- **Licensing storefront** (sync licenses)
- **Hardcore fan community** (Bandcamp Friday is a thing)
- **High-quality downloads** (lossless, WAV)

### Bandcamp Pricing Tiers (Recommended for Harmattan Sessions)
- **Individual tracks:** $1.50 minimum (Bandcamp's standard floor)
- **EP (5 tracks):** $5 minimum
- **Compilation album (10+ tracks):** $7-10 minimum
- **Lossless WAV / FLAC bundle:** $15-20
- **Sync license — Personal use:** $29 (instructions in EPIC-06)
- **Sync license — Commercial:** $79
- **Sync license — Extended:** $199

### Bandcamp Discovery Boost (One-Time Setup)
1. Tag everything with at least 8 tags (Afrobeat, Lofi, Highlife, Ambient, etc.)
2. Use the "by location" field — set to Accra, Ghana
3. Fill out artist bio with cultural depth
4. Pin a "Start Here" track on artist page
5. Upload at least one free track (Bandcamp's free downloads drive discovery)

## Playlist Submission Workflow

The realistic targets (in order of effort/return ratio):

### Free / High-Return
1. **Spotify for Artists "Pitch a Song"** — once per release
2. **DailyPlaylists.com** — submit to 10+ Afrobeat / Lofi playlists
3. **Direct curator email** — find via artist.tools, send personalized pitch
4. **Submithub free credits** — limited but valuable

### Paid / Moderate-Return ($1-50 per release)
1. **SubmitHub credits** — $1-5 per submission
2. **Indiemono** — $20/month, focuses on indie playlist curators
3. **Soundplate Clicks** — $25 for guaranteed reviews from playlist curators

### Avoid (Mostly Scams)
- Anything promising "guaranteed Spotify playlist" placement for fee
- "Buy 10k streams for $20" services (botted, will get you banned)
- Generic "music PR" services on Fiverr for under $50

## The "Awards Year" Strategy

Once you decide to pursue AFRIMA / Headies / Grammy seriously (typically Year 2):

### 12 months before submission window:
- Identify SIGNATURE track or compilation album
- Polish to extreme level (consider professional mastering engineer)
- Build streaming traction (target: 100k+ streams)
- Build press evidence (target: 3+ published pieces)
- Build editorial playlist evidence (target: 2+ confirmed placements)

### 6 months before submission window:
- Hire short-term PR firm if budget allows ($500-2000/month)
- Submit to ALL adjacent awards as practice (Ghana Music Awards, etc.)
- Build relationships with industry contacts at events

### 3 months before submission window:
- Finalize submission package
- Cross-check eligibility requirements
- Submit with full documentation
- Follow up appropriately (one polite check-in after 30 days)

### Submission day:
- Submit to multiple awards same week (efficiency)
- Document submission for your tracking
- DO NOT publicize "I submitted to AFRIMA" — looks desperate

### Post-submission:
- Continue normal release cadence
- Don't obsess
- Whatever happens, learn and resubmit next cycle

## The Hardest Truth About Releases

Most releases do nothing. The math:

- 90% of independent music gets <1000 streams
- 99% gets <10,000 streams
- 0.1% breaks 100,000

But: catalogs compound. Track 50 might not work. Track 100 might. Track 200 might be the one that gets editorial pickup and unlocks everything else.

The strategy is volume + quality + patience. Do not despair if individual releases underperform. The catalog is the asset. Year 2 will see returns Year 1 can't.

Trust the process. Release on the cadence. Document everything. The numbers come.
