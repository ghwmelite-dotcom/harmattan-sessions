# Award & Recognition Criteria — Reference

The realistic targets for Harmattan Sessions and what it takes to win them. Based on May 2026 award cycles.

## Tier 1 — Achievable in Year 1-2

### AFRIMA (All Africa Music Awards) — Best Bet

**The body:** All Africa Music Awards, organized in partnership with the African Union. The most respected continental music award.

**Relevant categories for Harmattan Sessions:**

| Category | Eligibility Notes |
|---|---|
| Best Alternative Song — West Africa | Strong fit — "alternative" includes ambient, instrumental, downtempo |
| Best African DJ | Long shot but possible if Harmattan Sessions develops a DJ-mix identity |
| Best African Soundtrack | Requires song featured in film/series. Pursue licensing partnerships |
| Best Producer of the Year | Long shot for AI-assisted; defer to Year 2+ |
| Album of the Year | Out of reach for instrumental project; pursue compilation albums |
| Song of the Year — Inspirational | Possible for Ancestral Ambient tracks with positive thematic positioning |

**Submission process:**
- Window: Opens ~April, closes ~August each year
- Cost: Free submission via afrima.org/submission
- Format: 320kbps MP3 + ISRC + artwork + bio + lyrics (if applicable) + social links
- Process: Initial jury screening → public voting → final jury decision
- Result: Nominations announced October-November; ceremony Q1 following year

**What wins Best Alternative — West Africa:**
- Cultural specificity (must FEEL African)
- Production polish at international standard
- Distinctive artistic voice
- Track that's been released for >6 months with traction (streams, press)

**Realistic 2027 target:** Submit *"Palmwine Evening"* or *"Sankofa"* — your strongest highlife/ancestral tracks — for Best Alternative West Africa.

### The Headies (Nigerian Music Awards)

**The body:** Premier Nigerian music award, expanded to West Africa scope.

**Relevant categories:**

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Best Alternative Song | Direct fit if track has alternative/indie positioning |
| Producer of the Year | Pursue after 50+ tracks released |
| Best Recording of the Year | Production-focused — favors polish over popularity |
| Special Recognition Award | Discretionary — for cultural impact |

**Strategy:** The Headies skews mainstream Nigerian Afrobeats. Submit only your most accessible Afro-Soul Sunset tracks. Don't waste submissions on Ancestral Ambient — wrong room.

### Ghana Music Awards (3MG Awards)

**The body:** Premier Ghanaian award. Home turf for Harmattan Sessions.

**Relevant categories:**
- Highlife Song of the Year — strong fit
- Best Music Producer
- Hiplife/Hip-Hop — out of scope
- Reggae/Dancehall — out of scope

**Strategy:** Highest probability of recognition. Submit aggressively from year 1. A Ghana Music Award nomination instantly validates the "Harmattan Sessions" brand domestically.

## Tier 2 — Achievable in Year 2-3

### BET Awards — Best International Act (Africa)

**The body:** Black Entertainment Television (US), with international category for African artists.

**Relevant category:**
- Best International Act (Africa) — historically dominated by major Afrobeats stars
- Best International Flow — vocal/rap focused

**Realistic assessment:** Long shot for instrumental project. Pursue only after AFRIMA recognition + significant streaming numbers.

### MOBO Awards (UK)

**The body:** Music of Black Origin Awards (UK).

**Relevant categories:**
- Best African Music Act — competitive
- Best Producer — possible

**Strategy:** UK diaspora amplification is key. Build UK-based press features first (gal-dem, OkayAfrica UK, gal-dem, NTS Radio).

### Stereofox / Chillhop Awards (Indie tier)

**The body:** Independent lofi/chillhop industry recognition.

**Strategy:** These outlets explicitly flag and reject AI-generated music as of 2026 (Stereofox quote: "we make sure no AI slop makes it onto our playlist selection"). HOWEVER — they accept AI-assisted music with strong human curation and field recordings. Position Harmattan Sessions as "AI-assisted, human-produced" with the field recording layer as your differentiator.

## Tier 3 — Stretch Goals (Year 3+)

### Grammy — Best Global Music Album

**Eligibility:**
- 51%+ of album must be in non-English language OR be a recognized world music style
- Standalone "album" (not compilation) of 5+ tracks
- Released within Grammy eligibility window (Sept-Aug)
- Distributed to major DSPs (Spotify, Apple, etc. — DistroKid satisfies this)

**Strategy:** Year 2-3, release a curated 8-track album like *"Harmattan: The Album"* with one signature track per sound. Submit via NARAS membership ($100/year for voting member status).

### Grammy — Best Global Music Performance

Single-track category. Same eligibility window. Higher production bar.

### NAACP Image Awards — Outstanding World Music Album

Less competitive than Grammy but real prestige. Submit alongside Grammy submissions.

### Submission Mechanics

1. **Spotify for Artists (free, official):** After your first DistroKid release goes live, claim your artist profile. Submit unreleased tracks via "Pitch a song" feature — you have ONE shot per release.

2. **DailyPlaylists (free):** Submit to 10+ playlists per release via dailyplaylists.com. Curators are independent.

3. **SubmitHub (~$1-5 per submission):** Wider curator network. Worth it for premium releases (max 3 best tracks per month).

4. **Direct curator outreach:** Find playlist owners via tools like artist.tools, email them personally with track preview link + 3-sentence pitch.

```
Track: Labadi Sunset
Genre tag: Afrobeats, Lofi
Mood tag: Chill, Sunset

Description:
"Labadi Sunset" is a chilled afro-lofi instrumental capturing 
the warm, hazy quality of Accra's coastal evenings. Recorded

modern afrobeats production with traditional palmwine guitar 
warmth. Perfect for sunset moods, study sessions, and slow 
focus playlists. From the Harmattan Sessions catalog — an 
independent music project rooted in Accra, Ghana.

Co-write credit: N/A (instrumental, solo production)
Recording info: Original Accra field recordings layered with 
AI-assisted composition. Independent release, 2026.
```

Keep description under 500 characters. The first 100 are most important — editors skim.

### What Spotify Editors Actually Look For

From researching editor feedback and curator interviews:

1. **Sonic fit:** Track sounds like it belongs in their target playlist. Listen to the playlist BEFORE submitting.
2. **Production quality:** Pro-level mix, -14 LUFS, no clipping, proper stereo width.
3. **Distinct identity:** Track has SOMETHING that makes it identifiable in 5 sec.
4. **Cultural moment:** Connects to a zeitgeist (e.g., Afrobeats global wave 2024-2026).
5. **Artist story:** A real person, a real place — not anonymous content farm.

Harmattan Sessions checks ALL FIVE. Use this in pitches.
