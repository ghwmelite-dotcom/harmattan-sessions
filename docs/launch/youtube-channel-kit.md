## 4. The 5 Title Formulas

Every long-form upload uses **exactly one** of these patterns. Consistency is what teaches the algorithm what you are.

### F1 — Length-led (best for sleep/sleep-mix → Tier-1 watch-hours)
`[N Hours] of [Sound] | [Use Case] — Harmattan Sessions`
- "3 Hours of Afro-Lofi for Deep Focus — Harmattan Sessions"
- "8 Hours of Afrobeats Rain for Deep Sleep | African Ambient — Harmattan Sessions"

### F2 — Mood + Setting (best for editorial pickup)
`[Mood] [Sound] | [Setting] (No Ads)`
- "Late Night Highlife Chill | Palmwine Evening in Accra (No Ads)"
- "Slow Sunday Amapiano | Hotel Lobby Lounge (No Ads)"

### F3 — Geography-anchored (best for cultural-moat reinforcement)
`[Place] [Time] — [Sound] for [Use Case]`
- "Labadi Sunset — Afro-Soul for Coastal Evenings"
- "Jamestown Lighthouse — Slow Highlife for Reading"

### F4 — Use-case-led (best for SEO — searched terms)
`Music for [Activity] | [Sound] from Ghana 🇬🇭`
- "Music for Studying | Afro-Lofi from Ghana 🇬🇭"
- "Music for Yoga | Coastal Afro-House from Ghana 🇬🇭"

### F5 — Mix-curator (best for the bi-weekly mega-mix)
`Harmattan Sessions Vol. [N] — [Theme] | [Sounds]`
- "Harmattan Sessions Vol. 1 — Coastal Evening Mix | Afro-Lofi & Highlife"
- "Harmattan Sessions Vol. 4 — Three Hours of Rain | Afrobeats Rain & Ancestral Ambient"

**Title rules (override anything above):**
- ≤ 70 characters where possible (mobile truncation)
- Capitalize like a book title
- Never keyword-spam ("Best Relaxing Afrobeats Music for Studying Sleep Focus Relax")
- Don't title in languages you don't speak fluently (Twi/Yoruba/Ewe) — see SKILL.md anti-patterns
- "(No Ads)" only on sleep/study mixes you genuinely don't mid-roll — and only until monetization makes mid-rolls worth more than the retention you'd lose

## 8. Community Tab Strategy

Activates at 500 subs (or earlier with new YouTube rules — check). Post **once per week, Wednesdays 17:00 GMT** — mid-week engagement spike between Tuesday and Friday uploads.

**Post rotation (4-week loop):**

- **Week 1 — Open question.** *"Where did you study today?"* (or "what's your evening sound?") + a tile from the most recent mix.
- **Week 2 — Behind-the-scenes.** A field-recording snippet from Accra (a 15-sec clip of waves at Labadi or the harmattan wind at Aburi) + "this is going into Friday's mix."
- **Week 3 — Curation note.** Tease the next mix's *one* unique element ("Friday's mix uses a kora line from a Toumani Diabaté-style improvisation") and ask which sound subscribers want more of (poll: 8 sounds → vote).
- **Week 4 — Cross-link.** Spotlight a Spotify pickup or playlist placement, or a press feature. Reciprocate any creator who featured your work that month.

**Never:** beg for subscribes in the community tab. The post should give before it asks.

## 10. Sleep-Mix Specific Overrides

The Afrobeats Rain sound has its own playbook. Most channel defaults are inverted for sleep:

- **Length:** 3 hours **minimum** — ideally 6–8 for Tier-1 watch-hour acceleration.
- **Fade-in:** 60-sec gentle fade-in (never shock sleeping listeners awake).
- **Fade-out:** 90-sec gentle fade-out at the end (don't drop them off a cliff).
- **No chapters / timestamps** in the description (sleepers don't navigate).
- **No end-screen** (it appears on top of the video — wakes sleeping listeners).
- **No mid-rolls** (kill retention + waking risk; the watch-hour leverage is worth more than the ad cents).
- **No audio CTAs** ("subscribe" mentioned in audio = retention killer).
- **Title pattern F1 only**, with explicit hours: *"8 Hours of Afrobeats Rain for Deep Sleep | African Ambient — Harmattan Sessions"*.
- **Pinned comment:** the sleep-mix override above (gentle, no ask).

## 11. Anti-Patterns the Channel Must Avoid

From SKILL.md — copied here so we read them every time we hit "publish":

- ❌ Static image + Suno track + cliché title (= instant monetization rejection)
- ❌ "Lofi to study to" knockoff titles
- ❌ Tracks with no recognizable cultural element
- ❌ 16 tracks released in one batch with identical thumbnails
- ❌ Begging for subscribes in video audio (kills retention)
- ❌ Applying for YPP at 999 subs (look authentic — wait for the buffer)
- ❌ Subscribe pop-up watermark every 30 sec
- ❌ End-screen on a 3-hour sleep mix
- ❌ Using Twi/Yoruba/Ewe in titles you don't speak
- ❌ Sacred Adinkra symbols as aesthetic decoration
