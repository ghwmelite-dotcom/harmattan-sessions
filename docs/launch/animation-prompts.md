# Animation Prompts — Harmattan Sessions Sound Catalog

Image-to-video prompts for animating the 8 character anchors into looping YouTube backgrounds. Each scene has both a **Grok (Imagine/video)** prompt and a **Veo 3 / 3.1 Lite** prompt — pick the platform that fits your credit budget and quality need for the upload.

**Source anchors:** `public/visuals/stills/{sound}-anchor-v1.png`

---

## Universal Principles (Apply To Every Generation)

These rules override anything else. If a generation breaks one, reroll.

1. **The character must NOT move.** No facial expression changes, no head turn, no hand movement, no body shift. Akua is frozen — the world breathes around her.
2. **The camera must NOT move.** No zoom, no pan, no parallax, no dolly. Tripod-locked composition.
3. **Only environment moves, and only subtly.** Hair strands sway, mist drifts, water ripples, candle flickers, rain falls, curtain billows. Nothing dramatic.
4. **Motion must be loopable.** End frame should be visually similar to start frame. Cyclical motion (waves, fan, rain) loops naturally; one-direction motion (walking, falling object) does not.
5. **No new objects appear or disappear.** Only existing elements animate.

If you don't enforce these, you'll get character faces that morph between frames (uncanny), camera drift (motion sickness on long YouTube watches), or shot changes (breaks the meditation).

---

## Platform Notes

### Grok (Imagine / video mode)
- Conversational prompts work fine — write naturally
- **Aggressive on "subtle"** — Grok tends to over-animate. Use words like *barely perceptible, gentle, slow, almost imperceptible*
- Best for B-roll, secondary scenes, quick iterations
- Typical output: 6 seconds, 720p
- Strength: fast turnaround
- Weakness: less faithful to source image; character drift is common

### Veo 3 / 3.1 Lite
- Use **cinematographic language**: *locked tripod, static wide shot, no camera movement, ambient motion only*
- Respects source image far more faithfully — character stays consistent
- Best for hero clips (the opening 30 seconds of the YouTube video) and primary loops
- Typical output: 8 seconds (Veo 3), longer on 3.1 Lite
- Strength: photoreal, faithful, cinematic
- Weakness: slower, more expensive per second

**Recommended strategy:** Veo 3.1 Lite for the **primary 30-second hero loop** of each track (the opening shot the viewer first sees). Grok for variant B-roll if you want subtle changes across a long mix.

## Scene 1 — Afro-Lofi · Osu Rooftop Sunset

**Anchor:** `afro-lofi-anchor-v1.png`
**Motion hook:** *Akua's hair strands and the city light below*
**Loop duration target:** 8 seconds
**Aspect:** 16:9

### Grok prompt
> Subtle ambient animation of this still image. The young woman in the foreground is completely motionless — her body, head, hands, and facial features must not move at all. Only the environment breathes: a few strands of her locs sway gently in a slow Accra evening breeze, palm fronds in the distance shift slowly, distant traffic lights on the street below twinkle faintly, golden sunset light pulses very softly. The camera is locked on a tripod, no zoom, no pan, no parallax. Barely perceptible movement, meditative, slow, cinematic. Loopable.

### Veo 3 / 3.1 Lite prompt
> Static locked-tripod wide shot, no camera movement. The Ghanaian woman in the foreground is completely still, her face and body frozen like a painting. Only ambient environmental motion: 2-3 strands of her locs sway slowly in the breeze, distant palm fronds shift gently, faint headlight motion on the Osu street below, the golden sunset light intensity pulses almost imperceptibly. Cinematic, painterly, Studio Ghibli aesthetic. 8 second seamless loop. No parallax, no shot change, no character motion.

## Self-Critique

The biggest failure mode for these prompts is **character drift**: the model trying to "help" by adding facial micro-expressions or shifting the body position. Both Grok and Veo's image-to-video models have a strong prior toward animating characters. The mitigation is the repetition of "completely motionless" / "completely still" / "frozen pose" — say it twice per prompt. If the first generation still drifts, add `the character is a still painting, only the world around her moves` to the prompt and reroll.
